Investment thesis

Supports

  • AI and cloud data-center demand for high-capacity hard drives has driven a sharp recovery — gross margin rebounded from 18.0% in FY23 to 35.2% in FY25.
  • Revenue snapped back 38.9% in FY25 after two consecutive years of decline, alongside disciplined cost execution.

Risks

  • By far the widest price-to-model gap of the three names — current price implies the market is pricing in years of sustained AI-storage upside beyond what this model captures.
  • The business carried negative stockholders' equity in FY24, reflecting a historically leveraged, cyclical balance sheet.
  • Revenue and net income have swung wildly (-37% to +39% growth in consecutive years), making this one of the harder cash flow profiles to forecast with confidence.
